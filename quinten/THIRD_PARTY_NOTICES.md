## CBR / RAR decoder — provenance under review

The RAR 2.9 and RAR5 decompression code in `core/formats` (used for CBR comics) has a structure
and naming that follow RARLAB's UnRAR source, whose licence is neither Apache nor MIT, and RAR
compression has no public specification. **Its provenance is under review; it is not claimed to be
a clean-room implementation.** Until that is settled, CBR support should be treated as not cleared
for distribution.

## Test fixtures

Test books are generated by the scripts in `scripts/` from original text and from public-domain
text (Lewis Carroll, *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*). Optional external fixtures
(Project Gutenberg files, libarchive RAR test vectors) are downloaded at test time by
`scripts/fetch_external_fixtures.sh` and are not part of the repository.
