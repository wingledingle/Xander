Privacy Policy

Xander Files (com.xander.files) · Effective 17 November 2026

In short: Xander Files works on your phone. We (the developer) don't collect, receive or sell any of your data: there are no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your files, the file index, thumbnails and settings stay on your device. Data only goes to a service when you connect that service yourself (for example a cloud drive, a network share, or a Cast device), and then it goes directly between your phone and that service.

1. Who we are

Xander Files is a file manager for Android, published on Google Play by its developer ("we"). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Data we collect

None. Xander Files has no server of ours that it talks to, no analytics or tracking libraries, no advertising, and no crash or usage reporting. We never see your files, your accounts or how you use the app.

3. What the app does with data on your device

  • Your files. With your permission ("All files access"), the app lists, opens, copies, moves, compresses, encrypts and deletes files you choose. It builds an index of file names, sizes and dates on the device so that search, the Library categories and storage analysis are fast. The index is stored only in the app's private storage.
  • Thumbnails and previews are made on the device and cached in the app's private storage.
  • Adult-content detection (off by default). If you switch it on, a machine-learning model that is built into the app classifies your photos and videos on the device to sort them into a private section. Nothing is uploaded; the results are stored only on the device and can be cleared in Settings.
  • Installed apps. The app manager lists the apps installed on your phone (to back up, share or uninstall them) and, if you grant "Usage access", shows how much storage each one uses. This information is only shown to you.
  • Encrypted files and protected archives use a password you choose. While such a job is waiting or running, the password is kept only in encrypted form (so the job can resume) and it is erased when the job finishes.
  • Settings, favourites, recent files and the recycle bin are stored on the device.

4. When data leaves your device (only when you choose)

The access tokens are stored on the device, encrypted with a key held by Android's Keystore. The provider's own privacy policy applies to data you store with it.

Built-in FTP and web servers (off until you start them) While running, devices on your local network can browse and transfer the folder you share, with the password you set (or without one if you choose anonymous access). Data goes directly between the devices.
Casting (Google Cast) The media you cast is streamed from your phone over your local network to the Cast device you pick, using Google's Cast software (Google Privacy Policy).
Bluetooth and the TV companion Files you send go directly to the device you pick.
Premium subscription Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only whether a subscription is active, never your payment details (Google Privacy Policy).
Sharing and "Open with" Files you share go to the app you pick.

6. Backups

The app's data is excluded from Android backup and from phone-to-phone transfer, so the file index, detection results and saved logins are not copied to Google's backup or to another phone.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device. You can remove accounts and network locations in the app, clear the index, cache and detection results in Settings, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Files in cloud drives or on servers stay there; manage them with the provider.

8. Children

Xander Files is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
